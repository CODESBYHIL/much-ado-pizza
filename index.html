The Company

Legend says Shakespeare hid the perfect pizza recipe in a play. In 2022, a Pleasanton theatre teacher cracked it.

“Baking bread was fun, but it wasn’t a bakery I wanted. It was a pizza shop.”

No sugar and no preservatives in the dough or the sauce. Local vegetables, Italian cheeses, a long cold rise.

Through Shakespeare Shares, pies go to Goodness Village, Open Heart Kitchen and CityServe of the Tri-Valley.

Members of the U.S. Pizza Team and the Pleasanton Chamber of Commerce.
